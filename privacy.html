Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Versión en español

Comesílabas: Aprende a Leer ("the app," "we") is an app that helps children ages 4 to 7 learn to read in Spanish. Because children use it, we designed it to collect nothing. This policy explains, in plain terms, what information the app handles and what it doesn't.

Summary

Comesílabas does not collect, transmit, or store personal data from anyone, neither the child nor their family. The app doesn't connect to any server of ours: the child's progress is stored only on the device itself, using iOS local storage (UserDefaults). We never see that information.

What information stays on the device

  • The stars earned in each lesson along the path.
  • The level reached in each game.
  • Whether the family has Comesílabas Pro and until when, so lessons open even when the device is offline.

None of this data identifies the child or leaves the device. We don't keep copies anywhere.

What the app never asks for

  • It doesn't ask for a name, age, email, photos, or location.
  • It doesn't use the microphone or the camera. The voices you hear are recorded inside the app.
  • It doesn't require creating an account.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Comesílabas shows no ads, uses no analytics or ad-tracking tools, includes no third-party SDKs, and doesn't share information with social networks or advertisers.

Parental gate

Purchases, links that leave the app (such as this page), and adult settings are behind a parental gate: a challenge a young child can't solve.

In-app purchases (Comesílabas Pro)

Comesílabas Pro purchases are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit), using the adult's Apple Account. We don't receive or store any payment or account information: Apple manages it under its own privacy policy.

How to delete the data

Since everything lives only on the device, the family is in full control:

  • In the app: Para papás (For parents) → Borrar progreso (Delete progress) deletes the stars and levels (Comesílabas Pro is not lost).
  • Uninstalling the app makes iOS permanently delete all of its data. Purchases aren't lost; they come back with "Restore purchases."

Because we have no access to this information, we can't view, correct, or delete it from any server. Even so, you can write to us with any request about personal data (access, correction, deletion, or objection) and we will respond.

Children

Comesílabas is directed at children and follows the rules of the App Store Kids category and children's privacy laws such as COPPA (United States). Because it collects no personal information of any kind, it doesn't need to request parental consent to collect it.

This website

These legal pages don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking tools.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Controller and contact

Comesílabas is operated by Omar Mascorro Godinez (independent developer, Mexico). If you have questions about this policy or your data, write to us at comesilabas.soporte@outlook.com.
